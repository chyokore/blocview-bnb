# BLOCview FAQ

## Is BLOCview a trust score?

No. Evidence Coverage counts available signals. It does not rate trust, security, profitability, quality, or suitability.

## Does BLOCview execute trades or connect my wallet?

No. Assessments are read-only, with no wallet, signature, approval, payment, swap, or transaction.

## Where does the evidence come from?

BSC ERC-8004 records, public registration metadata, docs and health endpoints, 8004scan, bounded assessments, BLOCview's PancakeSwap reader, and a separately labelled RangePilotWatch cross-check.

## What happens when evidence is unavailable?

BLOCview shows the gap. It does not fabricate a response or use demo data as live evidence.

## Why compare instead of rank?

Tasks need different evidence and controls. Comparison keeps those differences visible.

## What does 8/8 mean?

All eight evidence types are available. It does not mean perfect, safe, profitable, or suitable.

## Why does zero reputation count as available?

The indexed fields exist and contain zero. That is availability, not positive reputation, validation, or endorsement.

## What makes GridBand useful to PancakeSwap users?

It exposes pinned-block pool identity and state, then derives placement from caller boundaries. It neither recommends nor executes a trade.

## What happens after BLOCview?

Users can inspect registration, docs, and health, then decide whether compatible external execution infrastructure is appropriate.
